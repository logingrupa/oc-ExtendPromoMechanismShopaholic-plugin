Bundle price

Quantity is the number of units in one bundle. Target price is the price of the whole bundle, not of one item.

For "2 pcs for 35 EUR" enter quantity 2 and price 35.00. For "any 3 for 35 EUR" enter 3 and 35.00; the units cost 11.67 + 11.67 + 11.66, exactly 35.00.

Units from different offers and different cart lines count together, the most expensive units fill the bundles first. The deal repeats for every full bundle: 4 units make 2 bundles, and a unit left over keeps its normal price until the next one completes a bundle. A unit that already costs less than its bundle share keeps its own price.

Keep the campaign scope to the exact offers of the deal. Every offer in scope counts toward a bundle, so a whole product with 5 ml and 15 ml offers mixes both sizes.

Discount type, offer limit and "Quantity limit at which the discount will be repeated" are ignored for this type.
